  3. Anytime exploration for multi-armed bandits using confidence information
    Kwang-Sung Jun and Robert Nowak
    In Proceedings of the International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML)
    post-publication note

    Emilie Kaufmann and her group have pointed out an error in this paper. Specifically, the paper’s Lemma 7 and 8 is likely to be false. In an attempt to fix this, I realized that the stopping time’s tail probability should be controlled exponentially rather than polynomially. This led to our ICML’25 “Fixing the Loose Breke” paper that guarantees an exponential tail. We have not tried hard to see if this helps resolve the issue we had in the original paper, though.
